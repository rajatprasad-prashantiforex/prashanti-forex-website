Treasury Advisory & Policy Governance

Board-Approved Risk Charters, Delegation Matrices & FEMA Regulatory Governance

Board-Level Governance and Regulatory Compliance for Corporate Foreign Exchange Operations

Without a formal Board-approved Treasury Policy, companies expose promoters and directors to unchecked currency risk, unauthorized dealer decisions, and costly FEMA regulatory penalties. Prashanti Forex establishes institutional governance.

Many growing Indian mid-market enterprises operate treasury informally through verbal phone authorizations between finance controllers and relationship managers. This lack of structural documentation leaves audit committees vulnerable, exposes organizations to rogue derivative transactions, and creates major compliance vulnerabilities under Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Foreign Exchange Management Act (FEMA) Master Directions.

Prashanti Forex authors and implements institutional Treasury Risk Policies tailored to your enterprise scale. We define clear delegation of financial power (DoFP) matrices, set permissible hedging instruments, establish counterparty bank credit limits, and implement rigorous four-eye verification controls. Our guidance gives Board Directors complete confidence that risk is controlled, measured, and fully documented.

Key Deliverables in Treasury Advisory & Governance

Board-Approved Treasury Risk Policy

Drafting formal risk charters detailing exposure limits, authorized instruments, hedge ratios, and escalation pathways for Audit Committees.

Delegation of Authority (DoFP) Matrices

Establishing clear authorization limits between finance managers, CFOs, and Managing Directors to eliminate operational loopholes.

Banking Consortium Restructuring

Strategic advisory on consortium banking allocation, RFP design for new facilities, and counterparty exposure diversification.

FEMA & RBI Master Direction Compliance

Ensuring full compliance with underlying trade exposure rules, derivative contract documentation, and foreign asset/liability reporting.

Our 4-Stage Governance Formulation Process

01. Audit

Assessing existing decision flows, sign-off thresholds, derivative books, and FEMA documentation.

02. Drafting

Formulating the Treasury Risk Charter, permissible derivative lists, and stop-loss boundaries.

03. Adoption

Presenting the policy framework to the Board of Directors and Audit Committee for formal sign-off.

04. Oversight

Periodic compliance checks, regulatory audits, and policy updating as macro conditions evolve.
