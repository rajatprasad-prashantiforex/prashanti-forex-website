Frequently Asked Questions

Get started now Get started now
  • What is Fractional Treasury?
    Fractional Treasury is an outsourced corporate treasury model where professional treasury experts work as an extension of your organisation, managing agreed or complete treasury functions without the fixed cost and complexity of building an entire internal department.
  • Why should established businesses outsource their treasury?
    Outsourcing provides immediate access to specialised institutional foreign exchange and trade finance expertise, structured risk policies, and bank rate benchmarking without incurring the high overhead of hiring full-time senior treasury personnel.
  • Can you work alongside our existing finance team?
    Yes. Prashanti Forex works closely with your existing CFO, Finance Director, and accounts team. We complement your internal capabilities by bringing market intelligence, hedging discipline, and bank negotiation expertise to your daily operations.
  • How does Fractional Treasury improve profitability?
    By professionally managing foreign exchange risk to protect export/import margins, auditing and reducing banking borrowing/trade finance charges, and optimizing working capital liquidity, treasury becomes a measurable contributor to business profitability.
  • What business size is ideal for Prashanti Forex services?
    Our solutions are designed specifically for established exporters, importers, and corporate enterprises with an annual turnover of ₹100 Cr+ that have significant cross-border transactions and currency exposure.
