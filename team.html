Rajat Prasad

Founder & CEO (CTM | CAIIB)

Certified Treasury Professional | Treasury, Forex & Risk Management Specialist

Rajat Prasad is a Certified Treasury Manager with 20+ years of market experience, bringing deep expertise in Treasury Operations, Foreign Exchange, Forex Derivatives, Currency & Interest Rate Risk Management, Hedging, Trading, and International Finance.

His professional career includes 7 years in Treasury Front Office and 5 years in General Banking with Bank of India, followed by 8 years in Information Technology at Bank of India and 11 years as Group Treasury Head with Abans Group and a leading export conglomerate. He possesses extensive hands-on expertise across FOREX Spot, Forwards, Swaps, Currency Options, structured hedging frameworks, and international-market dollar financing.

Rajat is also an esteemed member of the BSE Risk Management panel, guiding exporters and importers on navigating volatile currency cycles. He has conducted masterclasses for BSE Institute, Institute of Banking & Finance, National Institute of Bank Management (NIBM Pune), Dun & Bradstreet, ITM Business School, and Bankers Training College.
