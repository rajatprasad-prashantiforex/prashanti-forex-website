Building a Better Approach to
Corporate Treasury

Prashanti Forex is focused on Fractional Treasury Solutions for established businesses with significant international transactions. Our approach is built around responsibility: we work alongside management and finance teams to manage agreed treasury functions, protect operating margins from foreign exchange volatility, optimize trade finance costs, and strengthen liquidity. For businesses that prefer to manage treasury internally, we also provide specialised Treasury Advisory and corporate training.
Phase 01: Diagnostics & Policy

Understand & Strategise

Before executing any market transaction, we conduct a comprehensive diagnostic of your corporate operating model, cross-border trade exposure, and banking facilities to construct a robust, institutional-grade risk policy.
  • Transaction Cycle Audit: Detailed assessment of your cash flow horizons, import/export settlement timelines, and working capital cycles.
  • Granular FX Exposure Mapping: Clear quantitative breakdown of transactional, balance sheet, and economic foreign exchange risks across key currencies.
  • Tailored Treasury Policy Formulation: Designing formal board-approved policies specifying hedge ratios, stop-loss triggers, and permissible hedging instruments.
  • Banking Facility & Spread Benchmarking: In-depth audit of existing bank rate markups, sanction limits, forward booking facilities, and interbank spreads.
Phase 02: Execution & Optimization

Manage & Improve

Acting as your dedicated outsourced Chief Treasury Officer and dealing desk, we operate alongside your internal finance team to execute disciplined hedges, lower banking spreads, and enhance continuous operational efficiency.
  • Daily Interbank Dealing Desk: Live rate discovery and transparent dealer support ensuring your banks execute at transparent institutional spreads (1–3 paise).
  • Disciplined Non-Speculative Hedging: Systematic forward bookings, options hedging, and active cancellation/re-booking management to protect operating margins.
  • Trade Finance Spread Reduction: Continuous negotiation of Letters of Credit (LC), Pre/Post-Shipment credit (EPC/PCFC), and Bank Guarantee (BG) charges.
  • Executive MIS & FEMA Compliance: Regular P&L mark-to-market reporting, exposure summaries, and board-level risk governance reports.
