Trade Finance & Borrowing Optimisation

EPC / PCFC Interest Spread Compression, Import LCs, Bank Guarantees & Working Capital Efficiency

Lowering Trade Finance Spreads and Bank Commission Costs Across Your Multi-Bank Facilities

International trade financing is burdened by opaque interest markups, unverified bank processing charges, and non-standard collateral fees. Prashanti Forex brings institutional rate intelligence to compress your borrowing costs.

Many corporations with sanctioned credit lines of ₹25 Cr to ₹250 Cr simply accept standard bank sanction terms without cross-bank competitive benchmarking. Between pre-shipment export credit in foreign currency (PCFC), rupee export credit (EPC), Letters of Credit (LC) opening commissions, and bank guarantee margins, unnecessary basis points accumulate into tens of lakhs in avoidable operational drag.

Drawing on senior institutional banking acumen (CTM | CAIIB), Prashanti Forex audits your sanction letters, security covenants, and pricing terms across all consortium and multiple banking partners. We help finance teams negotiate lower spreads, tap Interest Equalisation Schemes effectively, transition foreign currency borrowings to SOFR/EURIBOR baselines at optimal spreads, and structure buyer's credit facilities that conserve cash.

40–80 bps Interest Spread Savings
30%+ LC/BG Fee Reduction
100% Debit Memo Audited

Key Deliverables in Trade Finance Optimisation

PCFC & EPC Spread Benchmarking

Optimizing borrowing spreads on Pre-Shipment & Post-Shipment credit in USD, EUR, and GBP against international benchmark rates.

LC & Bank Guarantee Fee Rationalization

Benchmarking and renegotiating LC issuance charges, confirmation spreads, handling commissions, and performance BG margins.

Structured Supplier & Buyer’s Credit

Structuring overseas trade credit and factoring facilities to extend payable tenors while minimizing currency and interest risk.

Bank Charge & Debit Memo Audits

Continuous line-by-line verification of bank debit advices, SWIFT fees, and processing charges to recover overcharged fees.

4-Stage Trade Finance Optimization Roadmap

01. Review

Comprehensive audit of sanction letters, interest grids, and bank fee schedules across all banking partners.

02. Benchmark

Comparative peer analysis showing competitive rate bands across public, private, and MNC banks.

03. Restructure

Assisting your finance team in renegotiating spreads, covenants, and collateral allocation with lenders.

04. Monitor

Ongoing monthly audits of transaction debit advices to ensure negotiated pricing terms are honored.
