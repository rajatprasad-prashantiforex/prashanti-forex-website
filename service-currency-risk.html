Currency Risk Management & Hedging

Quantitative Foreign Exchange Exposure Mapping, Structured Derivatives & Operating Margin Protection

Protecting Operating Margins from Foreign Exchange Volatility Without Speculative Risk

Foreign exchange swings can wipe out an entire year’s operating profit in a matter of weeks. Prashanti Forex introduces institutional hedging discipline that decouples commercial profitability from currency market gyrations.

Most Indian mid-market exporters and importers approach forex reactively—hedging when the rupee is depreciating or panic-cancelling contracts at market bottoms. This absence of a disciplined framework transforms manufacturing, export, and distribution operations into inadvertent currency trading desks.

Prashanti Forex establishes a mathematical, policy-driven hedging architecture. We map your gross currency inflows and outflows across USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, and AED, identify natural netting opportunities, and formulate dynamic hedge ratio matrices. By combining forwards, options (collars, seagull structures, risk reversals), and strict stop-loss rules, we lock in your target costing rates while retaining flexibility for upside participation.

Core Currency Risk Deliverables

Cash Flow & Balance Sheet Exposure Mapping

Detailed mapping of firm commitments, forecasted order books, and foreign currency debt to compute true net Value at Risk (VaR).

Derivative Structuring & Plain Options

Custom structuring of cost-effective hedge instruments (put/call options, participating forwards) matching your cash conversion cycle.

Benchmark Stop-Loss Execution

Pre-defined trailing stop-losses and target profit realization triggers to remove human emotion from currency management.

Bank Forward Margin Auditing

Live verification of forward premia/discounts against interbank swap points to eliminate hidden bank markups on rollover and cancellation.

Our 4-Step Risk Mitigation Framework

01. Mapping

Quantifying open positions, export order timelines, import payables, and cross-currency exposure baskets.

02. Strategy

Establishing dynamic hedge ratios (e.g. 50% fixed forward + 30% option cover + 20% spot buffer).

03. Execution

Direct live market dealing, bank price discovery, spread shaving, and confirmation audits.

04. Governance

Weekly MTM statements, hedge performance reviews, and continuous scenario stress-testing.
